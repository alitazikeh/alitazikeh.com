About Me

Thoughtful design, built together.

I’m an Interactive Media Design graduate of Algonquin College who enjoys connecting with people through creative projects. I value kindness, clear communication, and collaboration.

I bring visual design and front-end development together to create useful digital experiences, and I’m always curious to learn what will make them better.

I am naturally curious and enjoy learning new skills, exploring new ideas, and challenging myself to grow. I take pride in being reliable and supportive, and I believe clear communication matters as much as strong results.

I enjoy working with others, sharing ideas, and contributing to projects where creativity and teamwork come together. Whether in a classroom or a professional setting, I aim to make the process positive for the people around me.

What Stands Out
  • Friendly and dependable collaborator
  • Strong visual taste with practical structure
  • Comfortable moving between design and front-end execution
