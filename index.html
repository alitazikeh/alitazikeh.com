About Me

Creative work grounded in kindness, curiosity, and reliable collaboration.

I am an Interactive Media Design graduate of Algonquin College who enjoys connecting with people through creative projects. I value kindness, respect, and positive collaboration, and I bring a calm, friendly energy into every environment I work in.

I am naturally curious and have a strong passion for learning new skills, exploring new ideas, and challenging myself to grow both personally and professionally.
